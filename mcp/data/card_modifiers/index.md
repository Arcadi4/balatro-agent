# Balatro Card Modifiers Reference

Modifiers are upgrades applied to cards that improve scoring, generate consumables or money, or manipulate cards. There are **4 modifier types**:

| Type         | Applies to                                    | Sub-page                                 |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Enhancements | Playing cards (one each)                     | `balatro://card_modifiers/enhancements` |
| Seals        | Playing cards (one each)                     | `balatro://card_modifiers/seals`        |
| Editions     | Playing cards, Jokers, Consumables (one each) | `balatro://card_modifiers/editions`     |
| Stickers     | Jokers in an active run (multiple)           | `balatro://card_modifiers/stickers`     |

Consumables (Tarot, Planet, Spectral) only ever take a Negative edition, granted by Perkeo. Vouchers and Booster Packs can never have modifiers.

## General rules

- A modifier stays with its card for the rest of the run. Applying an Enhancement or a Seal to a card that already has one replaces it; Editions and Stickers are permanent and cannot be changed.
- Modifiers are applied with Tarot or Spectral cards, or picked up in Booster Packs, or bought in the Shop once the Illusion voucher is owned.
- Debuffed cards and Jokers have every modifier effect disabled, with two exceptions: Negative still grants its +1 slot, and Stone cards still have no rank or suit. A debuffed Wild card counts only as its original suit.
