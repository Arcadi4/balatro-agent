Purchases a Joker — or a regular playing card (which appears in the shop once the Magic Trick voucher is active) — from the shop. Jokers go to an empty Joker slot; playing cards go into your deck. The card's cost is deducted from your money. Use this for any shop item shown as kind=joker or kind=playing_card in balatro_inspect_game_state. Requires SHOP phase, an affordable card, and room for a non-Negative Joker. For Tarot/Planet/Spectral cards use balatro_buy_consumable, for Vouchers use balatro_buy_voucher, and for Booster Packs use balatro_buy_booster.
