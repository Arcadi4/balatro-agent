Purchases a consumable card (Tarot, Planet, or Spectral) from the shop and immediately uses it in a single atomic action, applying its effect to the game state and bypassing the consumable slot entirely. Use this when you want to spend dollars on a consumable purely for its immediate effect — for example, buying a Tarot to enhance specific hand cards via the optional targets array, or buying a Planet to upgrade a poker hand level. Requires SHOP, an affordable Tarot/Planet/Spectral shop card, and valid targets only when that consumable operates on specific cards.
