Sells a card (Joker or consumable) from your slots for its sell value in dollars, permanently removing it from your possession. Use this when you need cash to buy a better card from the shop, when a Joker no longer fits your build, or when you need to free up a slot for an incoming card. Selling is available in stable interaction phases including blind select, hand selection, cash-out, and shop; it is not available during scoring/draw animations or while resolving Booster Packs. Requires a sellable Joker or consumable card_id in your slots; playing cards in hand cannot be sold this way.
