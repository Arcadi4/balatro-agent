Cashes out the round-end rewards (blind reward, interest, hand and discard bonuses, and any per-Joker dollar effects) into your bankroll and transitions the run from ROUND_EVAL into the SHOP phase. Use this immediately after defeating a blind when the game is presenting the cash-out screen and you are ready to enter the shop. Requires ROUND_EVAL after end-of-round effects have resolved; this is irreversible in non-endless runs.
