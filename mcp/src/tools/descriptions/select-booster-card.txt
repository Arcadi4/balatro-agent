Selects a card from an open Booster Pack. Jokers and playing cards are added to your collection; Tarot, Planet, and Spectral cards are used immediately and are not stored in consumable slots. Use this when a Booster Pack is open and you want to pick one of the revealed cards — for example, choosing a Joker from a Buffoon Pack, a Tarot from an Arcana Pack, or a playing card from a Standard Pack. Requires an open Booster Pack, remaining picks, a revealed pack card_id, and valid targets for targeted consumables. Death requires exactly two targets ordered source first and destination second.
