Purchases a Tarot, Planet, or Spectral card from the shop. Set use=true to buy and immediately apply the card's effect (bypassing the consumable slot entirely; pass targets for consumables that affect specific hand cards), or use=false to buy and store it in a consumable slot. The card's cost is deducted from your money. Requires SHOP phase, an affordable consumable, and — when use=false — a free consumable slot. When use=true, targets must be valid hand card IDs (Death requires exactly two, ordered source first then destination). Not every consumable can be applied immediately from the shop: hand-targeting Tarots/Spectrals are only usable during hand selection, so use=true on them fails with CANNOT_USE_NOW before any money is charged — retry with use=false to store the card and apply it later with balatro_use_consumable.
